# ffc-new

This is a production repository for the target-independent Lazy Fortran
driver and middle end. The laboratory in `../lazy-fortran-new` owns research
history, decisions, experiments, runs, provenance manifests, and
cross-repository wiring.

Keep this repository production-only. Do not add a local roadmap, research
ledger, model transcript, orchestration system, or copied external payload.
Use the versioned `mir-v0` contract from the laboratory. The middle end must
remain target-independent and must not import ISA or ABI details from
`fortback-new`.

General rules live in `~/code/prompts/AGENTS.md` and
`~/code/prompts/rules/fortran.md`.
